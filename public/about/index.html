About

Project Organizers and Consultants

The 2026 revision of this project was led by Llana Barber, Rudolph J. Vecoli Chair of Immigration History and the director of the Immigration History Research Center at the University of Minnesota.

This revised and updated #ImmigrationSyllabus was created by: Llana Barber, Rudolph J. Vecoli Chair in Immigration History and Director of the Immigration History Research Center, University of Minnesota; Carly Goodman, Assistant Professor of History, Rutgers University-Camden; Hidetaka Hirota, Associate Professor of History, University of California, Berkeley; Cecilia Márquez, Hunt Family Assistant Professor of History, Duke University; A. K. Sandoval-Strausz, Director of Latina/o Studies and Professor of History, Penn State University; Evan Taparata, Assistant Professor of History, University of Colorado - Colorado Springs; Michele Waslin, Assistant Director, Immigration History Research Center, University of Minnesota; Kimberly G. White, PhD Candidate in History, University of Pennsylvania; Shinya Yoshida, Graduate Assistant, Immigration History Research Center, and PhD Candidate in History, University of Minnesota.

Consultants included: Maddalena Marinari, Professor of History and the Dorothy Peterson, Mildred Peterson Hanson, and Arthur Jennings Hanson Endowed Professor of Liberal Studies, Gustavus Adolphus College; Yael Schacher, Director for Americas and Europe, Refugees International

Original 2017 Version

The original project was led by Erika Lee, past Director of the Immigration History Research Center and currently Bae Family Professor of History, Radcliffe Alumnae Professor, and the Carl and Lily Pforzheimer Foundation Director Schlesinger Library on the History of Women in America.

Organizers of the original #ImmigrationSyllabus included: Erika Lee, María Cristina García, Adam Goodman, Madeline Hsu, Julian Lim, Maddalena Marinari, and Evan Taparata.

Consultants for the original #ImmigrationSyllabus included: Katherine Benton-Cohen, Charlotte Brooks, John J. Bukowczyk, Donna Gabaccia, Libby Garland, David Gerber, Torrie Hester, Ely M. Janis, Anna D. Jaroszynska-Kirchmann, Kevin Kenny, Alan Kraut, Grainne McEvoy, Lucy Salyer, Jordan Stanger-Ross, and Elliott Young.

Contributors includ: María Cristina García, Howard A. Newman Professor of American Studies at Cornell University and President of the Immigration and Ethnic History Society (IEHS); Adam Goodman, Assistant Professor of History & Latin American and Latino Studies, University of Illinois at Chicago; Madeline Hsu, Professor of History, The University of Texas at Austin; Julian Lim, Assistant Professor of History, Arizona State University; Maddalena Marinari, Assistant Professor of History, Gustavus Adolphus College; and Evan Taparata, PhD Candidate in History, University of Minnesota.
